Client Brand Assistant for Designers

Case study

HDDesign’s designers needed fast, reliable answers about each client’s brand while they work. Over ten months we set up a multimodal language model for each client account, loaded with that client’s brand guidelines, tone-of-voice documents, and past campaign examples, so the team can use it as a daily partner on that account.

01 Client

A Kigali studio across many brands.

HDDesign Ltd was launched in Kigali in March 2013. It provides graphic design, website design and development, web applications, domain registration and hosting, and ongoing online maintenance for organisations in Rwanda.

Its graphic design team moves between client accounts during the day. Each account has its own guidelines, voice, and campaign history, and the work has to stay true to that brand every time.

02 Challenge

Brand knowledge in too many places.

Guidelines, tone-of-voice documents, and past campaigns sat in separate files and folders. Finding the right answer meant searching or asking a colleague who already knew the account.

New team members took time to learn each brand, and that knowledge was hard to pass on when people changed seats or accounts.

Designers needed answers while they worked: which logo version, which colour, which tone for a headline. Leaving the tool to dig through folders broke their flow.

03 Approach

One assistant per client brand.

For each client, we loaded the existing brand guidelines, tone-of-voice documents, and examples of past campaigns into a multimodal language model, so it can work with both text and images.

Designers ask it about colours, typography, logo use, wording, and how earlier campaigns handled a similar brief. It answers from that client’s material. Designers stay responsible for the work they produce.

We delivered over ten months, adding client accounts in stages and refining prompts, document structure, and the day-to-day workflow with the design team’s feedback.

04 How designers use it

Questions that belong to the brand.

A designer can ask for the approved palette on a campaign, paste a draft headline for a tone check, or share a rough layout and ask whether the logo treatment matches past work for that client.

The assistant is limited to the material loaded for that brand. It is not a general design chat; it is a working partner for one account at a time.

05 What we built

A daily partner for each account.

  1. 01

    Brand knowledge base

    Each client’s guidelines, tone-of-voice documents, and campaign examples, organised so the model can draw on them.

  2. 02

    Multimodal assistant

    Designers ask in text or share images, and the assistant responds with that brand in mind.

  3. 03

    Tone-of-voice help

    Suggestions for headlines and copy that follow the client’s documented voice.

  4. 04

    Campaign reference

    Quick access to how earlier campaigns for the same client looked and read.

06 Technology

What it runs on.

  • Multimodal LLM
  • Brand guideline documents
  • Tone-of-voice documents
  • Past campaign examples
  • Per-client workspaces

07 Outcome

Brand answers where the work happens.

  • Designers reach a client’s brand rules and campaign history from one place while they work.
  • New team members have a way to learn an account from its own material.
  • Each assistant is limited to the client it was built for.
