Process

Seven steps, each with a reviewable output.

Website projects fail most often through unclear content ownership and late surprises. Our process front-loads content and structure decisions so design and build proceed on firm ground.

  1. Discovery

    Goals, audiences, content audit, technical constraints, hosting, and languages. Output: project brief and sitemap.

  2. Wireframing

    Page structures and user journeys agreed before visual design, so navigation and content hierarchy are settled early.

  3. Design

    Visual system, components, and key page designs in light and dark variants where relevant, tested for contrast and legibility.

  4. Development

    Semantic, accessible, performant front-end; CMS or application back-end; integrations with forms, search, maps, or systems.

  5. Quality assurance

    Cross-device testing, accessibility audit against WCAG 2.1 AA, performance checks, security review, and content proofing in each language.

  6. Launch

    Hosting setup, DNS, TLS, redirects from the old site, analytics configuration if required, and a launch checklist signed off together.

  7. Support

    Editor training, documentation, and an agreed support arrangement for updates, security patches, and improvements.

Standards

What every site we deliver includes.

Accessibility

Semantic structure, keyboard operability, sufficient contrast, descriptive links and labels, and screen-reader testing. We target WCAG 2.1 Level AA and document any known exceptions.

Multilingual support

Content structures that handle Kinyarwanda, English, French, and Swahili cleanly — including language switching, translation workflows for editors, and correct language metadata for assistive technology and search engines.

Performance

Fast loading on modest devices and mobile connections: optimised images, minimal scripts, and sensible caching. Many of your visitors will be on phones; the site should work well for them.

Security

HTTPS everywhere, hardened CMS configuration, least-privilege editor roles, dependency updates, backups, and protection for forms against abuse.

Content ownership

Your team can edit every page without a developer. We train editors, document the content model, and avoid customisations that make upgrades painful.

Hosting you control

Sites are deployed to hosting accounts owned by you — local or international providers as policy requires — with credentials, backups, and renewal responsibilities clearly handed over.
