Accessibility Statement

Hanga Technologies Ltd is committed to making this website usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technology. This statement describes the standard we work to, what we have done, known limitations, and how to tell us about problems.

1. Our commitment

We want everyone to be able to read about our services and contact us without barriers. Accessibility is part of how we design and build, both for this website and for the systems we deliver to clients. We treat it as a quality requirement, not an optional feature.

2. Standard we work to

This website aims to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, motor, and cognitive impairments.

3. Measures we have taken

  • Semantic HTML structure with meaningful headings, landmarks, and lists, so screen reader users can navigate efficiently.
  • A "skip to main content" link at the start of every page.
  • All functionality available by keyboard, with visible focus indicators.
  • Text and interface colours tested for sufficient contrast in both light and dark modes.
  • Respect for the operating system's preference for reduced motion; animations are minimal and disabled when requested.
  • Text that can be resized up to 200% without loss of content or function, and layouts that reflow for small screens.
  • Form fields with visible labels, clear error messages linked to their fields, and instructions announced to assistive technology.
  • Descriptive link text; decorative graphics hidden from assistive technology.
  • Mobile menu and expandable sections built with proper states announced to assistive technology.

6. Compatibility with browsers and assistive technology

This website is designed to work with current versions of major browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) on desktop and mobile, and with common screen readers including VoiceOver, NVDA, and TalkBack. It does not require JavaScript to read content; JavaScript enhances the theme toggle, mobile menu, and contact form behaviour. The site may not display correctly in browsers that are no longer supported by their vendors.

8. Accessibility in our client work

We apply the same standard to the websites, portals, and applications we build for clients. Public-facing interfaces are designed to WCAG 2.1 Level AA, tested with assistive technology, and documented with any known exceptions. Multilingual content, including Kinyarwanda, is structured so that assistive technology can read it correctly. See our website development and approach pages for more detail.
