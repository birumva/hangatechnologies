About

A Rwanda-based technology company built for dependable delivery.

Hanga Technologies Ltd designs and delivers AI solutions, software, websites, and integrations for organisations that need systems to work reliably in real conditions. We are registered in Rwanda and based in Kigali, and we work with institutions, enterprises, NGOs, and growing businesses across the region.

Company overview

What Hanga does, and what it does not.

"Hanga" means to create in Kinyarwanda. The name reflects what we do: we build working systems for clients, rather than selling a single product. Our work spans AI-enabled workflows, custom web applications, institutional and company websites, and the integration work that connects new systems to the ones organisations already run.

We are a service-led delivery partner. Engagements are scoped around an operational problem, delivered in gated phases, and handed over completely. We do not operate a self-serve platform, and we do not sell subscriptions to tools. Where an existing product is the right answer for a client, we say so and help implement it.

We place particular emphasis on work in the public and regulated sectors, where governance, data protection, auditability, and multilingual service delivery are requirements rather than preferences. The habits this demands — careful discovery, documented decisions, human oversight of automation, thorough handover — benefit every client we work with.

Hanga Technologies Ltd is incorporated under the laws of Rwanda. Company registration details are available on our legal notice page.

Mission

Make capable technology dependable in the places it is needed most.

Advanced technology is widely available. What is scarce is the ability to make it work securely, in context, within existing institutions, and to keep it working. That is the gap we exist to close.

For institutions

Help public bodies and regulated organisations adopt AI and modern digital systems in a way their oversight functions can approve and their staff can trust.

For organisations

Give enterprises, NGOs, and growing companies systems that fit how they work, reduce manual effort, and remain maintainable without specialist teams.

For the region

Build local capability to deliver complex technology work to an international standard, from Kigali, for Rwanda and East Africa.

For the long term

Favour durable, well-documented systems over quick demonstrations, and leave every client more capable than we found them.

How we work

Small, senior teams and a structured method.

Projects are delivered by small teams with direct access to the people doing the work. The method is described in full on the approach page.

  1. 01

    Discovery before commitment

    We understand the workflow, data, constraints, and definition of success before proposing a solution or a price. If the right answer is smaller than expected, we say so.

  2. 02

    Decisions in writing

    Scope, architecture, oversight points, and acceptance criteria are documented and agreed. Changes go through the same process. Nobody should be surprised by what is delivered.

  3. 03

    Working software, regularly

    We demonstrate working systems on realistic scenarios throughout the build, so feedback arrives while it is still cheap to act on.

  4. 04

    Complete handover

    Code, infrastructure, documentation, and training are transferred fully. Clients own what they paid for and can take it elsewhere.

Values

Four commitments we hold ourselves to.

Clarity

Plain language in everything we write and build. Clear scope, clear decisions, clear interfaces. If something cannot be explained simply, we keep working on it.

Reliability

Systems that work under real conditions, delivered when we said they would be. Monitoring, backups, and recovery are part of launch, not afterthoughts.

Responsibility

Lawful, minimal handling of data. AI deployed with oversight and the means to switch it off. Honest advice, including when it costs us work.

Craftsmanship

Care in the details: accessible markup, clean data models, readable code, considered typography. The quality that is still visible years later.

Why local presence matters

Based in Kigali, working in your context.

Technology projects for institutions depend on understanding how things actually work: how decisions are approved, which systems are already in place, which languages staff and the public use, what connectivity looks like outside the capital, and how procurement cycles run. A local team learns these things by being present, not by reading about them.

Being in Kigali means we can meet in person, observe processes where they happen, respond in the same working day, and stay accountable after launch. It also means we are subject to the same legal and regulatory environment as our clients, including Rwanda's data protection framework.

At the same time, we hold ourselves to international engineering and design standards, use the same tooling and practices as teams anywhere, and work comfortably with international partners, donors, and vendors. Clients get local accountability without compromising on quality.

We are also committed to sustainable delivery: building systems that local teams can maintain, training client staff as part of every project, and contributing to the growth of technology capability in Rwanda.
