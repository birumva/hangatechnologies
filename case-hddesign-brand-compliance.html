Brand Compliance Review for Finished Designs

Case study

HDDesign produces flyers, social media posts, and packaging for many client brands, and each brand has its own rules. Over six months we built a review workflow in which a designer uploads a finished design and a vision-enabled language model reads the layout and text, checks it against that client’s indexed guidelines and campaign checklist, and reports what needs attention.

01 Client

Finished work that must match the brand.

HDDesign Ltd delivers graphic design and digital work for organisations across Rwanda from its studio in Kacyiru, Kigali.

Before a piece leaves the studio, it has to respect the client’s logo rules, colours, fonts, required wording, and the items on that campaign’s brief.

02 Challenge

Every brand has rules, every check took time.

Manual review depends on who is checking and how well they know the client. Small misses reach the client and cost a revision round.

Guidelines and campaign checklists lived in documents that reviewers had to open and search by hand while looking at the layout.

The studio needed a repeatable check that still left the designer in charge of what changes.

03 Approach

Check against the source, not from memory.

Each client’s brand guidelines and campaign checklists were indexed so the model retrieves the rules that apply to that brand, rather than relying on general knowledge.

A designer uploads a screenshot or export of the finished work. The vision-enabled model parses the layout and the embedded text, compares them with the retrieved rules, and lists each finding with the guideline it relates to.

The output is a review, not a verdict. The designer decides what to change, and the final sign-off stays with the team.

04 What gets checked

Layout, text, and the brief.

Typical checks include logo version and clear space, approved colours and fonts, required legal or campaign wording, and items from the campaign checklist that apply to that piece.

Findings are written so a designer can see what the model saw and which rule it used. Nothing is changed in the file automatically.

05 What we built

From upload to a clear review.

  1. 01

    Design upload

    Designers submit a PNG, JPG, or PDF of a flyer, social post, or packaging layout and choose the client.

  2. 02

    Visual parsing

    A vision-enabled model such as Claude or GPT-4o reads the layout, colours, logo placement, and embedded text.

  3. 03

    Guideline retrieval

    Retrieval-augmented generation pulls the matching rules from that client’s indexed guidelines and campaign checklist.

  4. 04

    Review report

    Findings are listed against the guideline they come from, so the designer can check each one quickly.

06 Technology

What it runs on.

  • Vision-enabled LLMs (Claude, GPT-4o)
  • Retrieval-augmented generation
  • Indexed brand guidelines
  • Campaign checklists
  • PNG, JPG, PDF input

07 Outcome

Brand checks that are consistent and traceable.

  • Every finished design can be checked against the client’s own rules before delivery.
  • Each finding points to the guideline or checklist item behind it.
  • Designers keep the final decision on every change.
  • Evidence of the engagement is available on request.
