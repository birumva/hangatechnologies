Medusa: Discovery and Reservations

Case study

Medusa is a Leipzig venue that brings together Alsatian food, cocktails, and cultural events. We built its website, which presents the venue, its menus, opening hours, happy hour, and contact details in one place, and an AI voice assistant that answers calls and takes booking requests.

01 Client

A bar where Alsace meets Leipzig.

Medusa serves traditional and reinterpreted Flammkuchen, French cocktails and spirits, and beer from a small local brewery, at Karl-Liebknecht-Straße 47 in Leipzig.

It is also a setting for art, music, and cultural performances. The website had to carry that character as well as the practical details a guest needs.

02 Challenge

A long menu and a ringing phone.

The menu spans eight categories, from Flammkuchen and tapas to cocktails, wine, and coffee. Presented as one list, it would be long and hard to navigate.

The telephone raised a different problem. A booking call has to capture accurate details, resolve ambiguity, and keep a reservation request distinct from a confirmed reservation.

We treated both as one customer journey: discover the venue, explore the menu, find the practical details, ask a question, request a booking.

03 Approach

Start from what a guest asks.

We structured the site around a guest’s questions: what kind of place is this, what is there to eat and drink, when is it open, where is it, and how do I get in touch.

The introduction explains the venue’s character first. The menu and the visit details follow, so the site works as a brand introduction and as a planning tool.

The menu interaction is expressive, so we paired it with a plain alternative. Guests who want to browse can move through the menu panel by panel; guests who want one category can go straight to it from the index.

04 Voice assistant

A second way to reach the venue.

Some guests prefer to call. Alongside the website, we created an AI voice assistant that answers incoming calls and takes booking requests.

Its role is deliberately narrow: understand why the customer is calling, collect what is needed to handle the request, and give a clear next step. It supports the venue’s team and does not make commitments on their behalf.

05 The website

Four parts of one visit.

  1. 01

    Venue introduction

    The link between Leipzig and Alsace, the food and drink, and Medusa as a place for art, music, and culture.

  2. 02

    Card-stack menu

    Eight panels, one per category. Guests swipe the top panel aside or use the arrow keys, and each panel scrolls on its own.

  3. 03

    Menu index

    A direct list of all eight categories for guests who know what they are looking for.

  4. 04

    Visit information

    Address, telephone number, opening hours, and happy hour together in one section.

06 Technology

What it runs on.

  • CSS
  • JavaScript
  • Swipe and keyboard navigation
  • AI voice assistant

07 Outcome

From discovery to a planned visit.

  • The venue, its menu, and the visit details sit in one customer-facing place.
  • All eight menu categories can be reached by swiping, by keyboard, or from the index.
  • Guests who prefer to call can ask questions and request a booking by phone.
