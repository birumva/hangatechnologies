Cooperative Supply Platform

Sample case study

A growers' cooperative recorded collections on paper and settled payments weeks later. We designed and built a field app and an office system that record each delivery once and show growers what they are owed.

This is a sample study that shows how we present our work. The client, figures, and details are illustrative and will be replaced by a published engagement.

01 Client

A cooperative owned by the people who grow.

The cooperative collects produce from member growers at stations across the hills and sells it on to processors.

Members are paid by weight and grade. Trust depends on those records being right and being seen.

02 Challenge

Paper slips, late payments.

Collection clerks wrote each delivery on a slip. Slips were retyped at the office, sometimes days later, and disputes were hard to settle.

Collection stations have little or no signal. Many growers use basic phones, so receipts had to reach them by SMS.

03 Approach

Design in the field, with the clerks.

We spent time at collection stations before drawing a screen. The first prototype was tested by clerks during a real collection day.

The field app was built to work fully offline. The office system came second, once the field records were reliable.

04 What we built

One record, from scale to payment.

  1. 01

    Collection app

    Weigh, grade, and record a delivery in a few taps, with or without a connection.

  2. 02

    Grower receipts

    An SMS to the grower for every delivery, with weight, grade, and running total.

  3. 03

    Office ledger

    Deliveries, advances, and payments in one ledger, ready for mobile money payout.

  4. 04

    Transport planning

    Daily volumes by station so trucks are sent where the produce is.

05 Technology

What it runs on.

  • Kotlin
  • Offline sync
  • Node.js
  • PostgreSQL
  • SMS gateway
  • Mobile money API

06 Outcome

Records growers can check.

  • Each delivery is recorded once, at the scale, and confirmed to the grower by SMS.
  • Office staff settle payments from the ledger instead of retyping slips.
  • Disputes are resolved against a shared record.
  • The cooperative owns the system, the data, and the documentation.
