Services

Technology services scoped around operational outcomes.

We work across software, AI, data, design, and infrastructure. Engagements are shaped by the problem to be solved — a slow approval process, a website that no longer serves the public, a set of systems that do not talk to each other — rather than by a fixed product.

Service areas

Six areas of work, usually combined.

Each area below describes what we do, what you receive, and when it is a good fit. Use the anchors to jump to a specific service.

01Software

AI Native Software Delivery

Web, mobile, and backend systems built by engineers who use AI tools at every step and review everything those tools produce. You get working software sooner, with the same standard of testing, security, and documentation.

Typical deliverables
  • Requirements and process documentation
  • Data model, API design, and role-based access control
  • Web and mobile applications with automated tests
  • Administration tools, reporting, and exports
  • Deployment, monitoring, and operational runbooks
Good fit when
A process runs on spreadsheets, email, and paper, or an off-the-shelf tool cannot be adapted to how your organisation actually works.
What we insist on
Human review of all AI-assisted code, well-supported technology choices, clear ownership of source code and data, and a design another team could maintain.
02Integration

Platform Modernisation

Replacing or connecting the systems that slow operations down: records and registry systems, finance and HR platforms, payment gateways, identity services, and partner APIs. Data moves reliably and is recorded once.

Typical deliverables
  • Assessment of current systems and a phased replacement plan
  • Integration architecture and interface specifications
  • Secure API gateways, connectors, and data synchronisation jobs
  • Data migration, mapping, validation, and reconciliation reports
  • Interface documentation for each connected system
Good fit when
Staff re-enter the same information into several systems, reports need manual consolidation, or a legacy platform can no longer be supported.
What we insist on
No big-bang cutovers, agreed data ownership, encrypted transport, scoped credentials, and full visibility into what moved where and when.
03AI

AI & Data Engineering

Practical AI applied to specific workflows, on data you can trust: processing documents, routing requests, answering questions from approved sources, and supporting decisions, with human review where it matters.

Full AI solutions overview

Typical deliverables
  • AI readiness assessment and use-case prioritisation
  • Data pipelines, quality checks, and a documented data model
  • Document classification, extraction, and validation workflows
  • Supervised multi-step workflows with permissions and escalation rules
  • Evaluation sets, test results, audit trails, and monitoring dashboards
Good fit when
Staff spend significant time on repetitive reading, sorting, data entry, or lookup, and the inputs and rules are reasonably well understood.
What we insist on
Defined human review points, logging of every automated decision, privacy-preserving data handling, least-privilege access, and a tested fallback when the AI component is unavailable or uncertain.
04Design

Product Design

Research, interface design, and build for products, portals, and websites. We take an idea from its first design decisions to something real people can use, in the languages they speak and on the devices they own.

Website development in detail

Typical deliverables
  • User research, content audit, and information architecture
  • Wireframes, prototypes, and usability testing with real users
  • Visual design system and page templates
  • Multilingual structure (Kinyarwanda, English, French, Swahili as required)
  • Accessibility and performance testing, and editor training
Good fit when
You are launching a new product or service, or an existing one is hard to use, hard to update, or no longer reflects what the organisation does.
What we insist on
Testing with the people who will use it, semantic and accessible HTML, and content ownership by your team.
05Infrastructure

Cloud Migration

Moving systems to the cloud, or to better-run hosting, on infrastructure sized for how you operate. We plan the move, carry it out in stages, and leave you with monitoring, backups, and costs you can see.

Typical deliverables
  • Inventory of systems, data, and dependencies
  • Target architecture, including data residency and security controls
  • Staged migration plan with rollback steps
  • Infrastructure defined as code, with monitoring and alerting
  • Backup verification, incident procedures, and cost reporting
Good fit when
Servers are ageing or unsupported, outages are hard to diagnose, or a new service needs capacity your current hosting cannot provide.
What we insist on
A hosting and backup arrangement you understand and control, tested recovery, and no lock-in. You can take the system elsewhere at any point.
06Advisory

Digital Transformation

Structured work to make the case for change and then carry it through: current-state assessment, options analysis, target architecture, and a costed, phased roadmap suitable for internal approval or procurement.

How we deliver

Typical deliverables
  • Stakeholder interviews and workflow mapping
  • Systems and data inventory
  • AI or digital readiness assessment
  • Phased roadmap with effort estimates and risks
  • Staff training, handover, and support through adoption
Good fit when
You need a defensible plan before budgeting, tendering, or approving a larger programme, or you want an independent view on a proposed solution.
What we insist on
Honest findings, including when the right answer is a smaller project, a process change, or an existing tool.

Engagement models

Ways of working that match your governance.

We adapt to procurement rules and approval cycles. Most projects begin with a discovery engagement so that scope and cost are grounded in evidence.

Discovery engagement

A fixed-duration assessment producing a clear problem statement, options, architecture, and a costed roadmap. Typically two to six weeks.

Fixed-scope delivery

A defined set of deliverables, acceptance criteria, and milestones. Suited to procurement-led projects and well-understood requirements.

Phased programme

Larger initiatives delivered in gated phases, each with its own scope and sign-off, so value is realised early and risk stays contained.

Retained support

An ongoing arrangement for maintenance, monitoring, and improvement, with agreed response times and a regular review cadence.

Scoping

How we scope work before quoting it.

Start from the workflow

We map how work moves today — who does what, with which systems and documents — before proposing where technology helps.

Name the constraints

Data protection requirements, existing platforms, connectivity, languages, budget cycles, and approval chains shape the solution and are captured in writing.

Define success measurably

Turnaround time, error rates, staff hours, service uptake, or accessibility conformance — agreed up front so outcomes can be verified.

Estimate with ranges and risks

Estimates include assumptions and the risks that could change them. Where uncertainty is high, we recommend discovery first.
