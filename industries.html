Industries

Sector experience that shapes how we scope and deliver.

Technology problems look similar across sectors; the constraints do not. Procurement rules, data sensitivity, connectivity, languages, and the people on the other side of the screen all change the right answer. Here is how we approach the sectors we work in most.

Government & Public Services

Ministries, agencies, districts, and public bodies delivering services to citizens and businesses, often across multiple languages and channels, within strict procurement and data governance frameworks.

Typical challenges

  • Paper and email processes with long turnaround times and backlogs
  • Websites organised around departments rather than citizen tasks
  • Systems that do not share data, forcing repeated submissions
  • Pressure to adopt AI with limited guidance on doing so safely
  • Accessibility and multilingual obligations that are hard to meet

How we help

  • Readiness assessments and phased roadmaps suitable for approval and tender processes
  • Supervised AI for intake, triage, and document review with full audit trails
  • Task-based, multilingual, accessible institutional websites and portals
  • Integration with existing national and institutional platforms
  • Documentation, training, and handover that respects public ownership of systems

NGOs & Development Programmes

Local and international organisations and donor-funded programmes working with implementing partners, field teams, and communities, with significant monitoring and reporting obligations.

Typical challenges

  • Data scattered across partners, spreadsheets, and collection tools
  • Slow, contested consolidation for donor reporting
  • Programme websites and knowledge products that are hard to maintain after funding cycles
  • Safeguarding and privacy requirements for beneficiary data
  • Limited in-house technical capacity between projects

How we help

  • Shared indicator frameworks and automated data consolidation
  • Permissioned dashboards for programme management and partner accountability
  • Privacy-by-design handling of sensitive beneficiary information
  • Sustainable websites and knowledge platforms with clear handover
  • AI-assisted summarisation and reporting with human review

Professional Services

Law, accounting, consulting, engineering, and advisory firms whose work depends on documents, approvals, client confidentiality, and accurate billing.

Typical challenges

  • Client onboarding and engagement approvals handled by email
  • Time spent reviewing and summarising long documents
  • Client data entered repeatedly across practice and finance systems
  • Websites that do not reflect the firm's actual expertise
  • Confidentiality concerns about using AI tools with client material

How we help

  • Internal workflow applications for onboarding, approvals, and matter management
  • Private, access-controlled document review and summarisation tools
  • Integration between practice management and accounting systems
  • Credible, well-structured firm websites
  • Clear AI usage policies and technical controls to back them

Education

Schools, universities, training providers, and education programmes managing admissions, records, learning content, and communication with students, parents, and staff.

Typical challenges

  • Admissions and registration processes dependent on paper forms
  • Student records spread across systems and spreadsheets
  • High volumes of routine inquiries from students and parents
  • Websites that are hard to navigate for prospective students
  • Need to protect minors' and students' personal data

How we help

  • Application and registration portals with document upload and status tracking
  • Integrated student information and records systems
  • AI-assisted inquiry triage drawing on approved institutional information
  • Accessible, multilingual institutional websites
  • Data protection controls appropriate to educational data

Trade & Commerce

Distributors, retailers, importers, exporters, and marketplaces coordinating orders, inventory, logistics, and payments across physical and digital channels.

Typical challenges

  • Inventory and prices inconsistent across sales channels
  • Manual reconciliation of mobile money, card, and bank payments
  • Order and delivery status invisible to customers and managers
  • Supplier and customer documents processed by hand
  • Limited reporting on margins and stock movement

How we help

  • Integration of e-commerce, point-of-sale, accounting, and payment systems
  • Automated payment reconciliation with exception handling
  • Order tracking portals and customer notifications
  • Document processing for invoices, delivery notes, and customs paperwork
  • Operational dashboards for stock, sales, and cash

SMEs & Startups

Growing companies that need a credible web presence, internal systems that scale beyond spreadsheets, and practical automation without an in-house technology team.

Typical challenges

  • Website that does not reflect the business or convert inquiries
  • Operations running on spreadsheets, chat messages, and memory
  • Founders spending time on repetitive administrative tasks
  • Uncertainty about which technology investments are worth making
  • Need for systems that can grow without being rebuilt

How we help

  • Clear, professional company websites with simple content management
  • Right-sized internal tools for the processes that matter most
  • Targeted automation of administrative work with sensible safeguards
  • Honest technical advice, including when an off-the-shelf tool is enough
  • Architectures that can scale as the business does
