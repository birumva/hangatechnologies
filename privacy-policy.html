We collect only the personal data needed to operate this website and respond to you.

Information you provide

  • Contact form and email inquiries: your name, organisation, email address, phone number (if provided), the type of inquiry, and the content of your message.
  • Business correspondence: information you share with us when discussing a project, tender, or partnership, including documents you choose to send.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs: our hosting provider may record your IP address, browser type, device information, the pages you visit, and the date and time of access, for security and operational purposes.
  • Preferences: your light or dark theme choice is stored in your browser's local storage. It is not transmitted to us.

We ask that you do not send us special categories of personal data (such as health, biometric, or political information) or confidential personal data about third parties through the contact form.

3. How we use personal data

  • To respond to your inquiry and communicate with you about it.
  • To prepare proposals, scopes, or tender responses you have requested.
  • To manage our relationship with you if you become a client or partner.
  • To operate, secure, and improve this website, including detecting and preventing abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations, including record-keeping and responding to lawful requests from authorities.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use personal data collected through this website for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Lawful basis for processing

We process personal data in accordance with Rwanda's law relating to the protection of personal data and privacy ([Law No. 058/2021 of 13/10/2021]) and other applicable laws. Our lawful bases are:

  • Consent, where you submit the contact form and agree to our use of your details to respond to you. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Contract, where processing is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, or to perform a contract with you.
  • Legitimate interests, for website security, operational logging, and managing business relationships, where these interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal obligation, where we are required to retain or disclose information by law.
