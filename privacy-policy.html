3. How we use personal data

  • To respond to your inquiry and communicate with you about it.
  • To prepare proposals, scopes, or tender responses you have requested.

4. Lawful basis for processing

We process personal data in accordance with Rwanda's law relating to the protection of personal data and privacy ([Law No. 058/2021 of 13/10/2021]) and other applicable laws. Our lawful bases are:

  • Consent, where you submit the contact form and agree to our use of your details to respond to you. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Contract, where processing is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, or to perform a contract with you.
  • Legitimate interests, for website security, operational logging, and managing business relationships, where these interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal obligation, where we are required to retain or disclose information by law.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

This website is designed to operate with essential technologies only. Your theme preference is stored in local storage on your device; no tracking cookies are set by default. If we introduce optional cookies, such as analytics, we will ask for your consent first and update our Cookie Notice, which contains full details.
